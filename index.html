Health analyst & writer  ·  Bilingual EN/ES  ·  Wellington & remote

Health analysis and writing, with a decade inside the clinic.

I am a bilingual (English/Spanish) health analyst and writer. I have ten years of frontline experience in New Zealand's oral health sector, postgraduate training in public health policy at distinction level, biostatistics, health promotion, and regulatory writing, and comparative work on the US, Singapore, and Aotearoa health systems. I can ]work on policy, regulatory, content, and systems projects for organisations that need health information to be both accurate and readable.

What I can work on

  • 01
    Oral health policy and the dental sector New Zealand's dental sector and the policy around it: workforce shortages, affordability barriers, fluoridation debates, and the day-to-day reality of how a clinic actually runs. Useful for dental practices, dental groups, regulators, and policy teams that need analysis grounded in clinical experience.
  • 02
    Health equity, Indigenous health, and Te Tiriti o Waitangi Practical work for organisations whose patients, members, or communities are reached least well by the health system. Te Tiriti and Māori health frameworks (Te Whare Tapa Whā, Te Pae Mahutonga) in Aotearoa; Indigenous health frameworks for international work; health equity and social determinants of health for US and global health buyers.
  • 03
    Regulatory writing and clinical compliance Health impact assessments, regulatory impact assessments, parliamentary submissions, and the operational documentation small healthcare practices need: risk registers, infection prevention and control, audit preparation, and incident reporting frameworks.
  • 04
    Quantitative methods Applied biostatistics, multivariable regression, model diagnostics, and translating statistical findings into language non-technical audiences can act on.
  • 05
    Global and comparative health systems Comparative analysis of high-income health systems, with particular attention to the US, ANZ, and Singapore. Useful for organisations planning international expansion, market entry, or system-level reform.
