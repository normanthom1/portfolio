Working together

Project work I am available for

A short list of the kinds of projects I am available to take on alongside the permanent and fixed-term roles I am applying for. My background is clinical, a decade as a dental assistant across multiple Wellington dental practices, and academic, a Postgraduate Diploma in Public Health from the University of Otago, completed with distinction. I am at an early stage in my freelance work, and the framing and pricing on this page reflect that.

Where I am in my freelance work

I want to be straightforward about this. My professional experience is clinical, not consulting. I have not previously held paid roles in health policy, health regulation, or health communications, and the analytical work on my Work page was produced during my postgraduate study rather than for clients. What I bring is academic capability that was assessed at distinction level, a decade of operational exposure to how a clinical setting actually runs, and a track record of writing about health clearly in two languages.

I would rather under-promise than over-promise. The offers below are sized to what I can credibly produce based on my training and clinical experience. Intro pricing reflects the fact that I am building a portfolio of commissioned work; my aim with first engagements is to do excellent work, earn the right to repeat or refer, and grow into the standard rates over time.

1. Patient education and clinical content

Patient-facing writing for healthcare practices: new patient guides, post-operative care instructions, treatment plan explainers, recall letters, website content, social media, and newsletters. The work is built on understanding both the clinical accuracy that practitioners need and the language that patients can actually act on.

What I bring. Ten years inside Wellington dental practices reading clinician notes, explaining treatment plans to patients, watching what works and what does not in patient communication. A Diploma in Cosmetic Dentistry (Universidad de Buenos Aires) and a Postgraduate Diploma in Public Health. Writing samples are available on my Blog page.

Best fit. Independent dental practices, allied health practices, and small health businesses that need content with clinical accuracy and that do not have an in-house writer.

Intro pricing for first clients. From NZD 500 per month for a small content retainer (one to two pieces), or from NZD 200 per individual piece. Standard rates will move up as I build case studies.

2. Bilingual English/Spanish health content

Health content produced in English and Spanish, or transcreation of existing English health materials into Spanish that reads as native rather than translated. Patient education, clinic communications, and explainers.

What I bring. Native Spanish (I grew up in Medellín, Colombia, and completed my dental training in Spanish), a decade of clinical English in Wellington practices, and a Postgraduate Diploma in Public Health. The combination is uncommon, most bilingual translators do not have clinical fluency, and most clinically fluent writers are not bilingual.

Best fit. ANZ clinics with Spanish-speaking patient populations; health publishers serving Latino patient audiences (including in the US, where the market for clinically accurate Spanish-language health content is large); Spanish-language health programmes needing English versions.

Intro pricing for first clients. From NZD 0.15 per word for transcreation, or from NZD 250 per packaged piece (one English and one Spanish version). Standard per-word rates will move up as I build case studies.

3. Evidence reviews, policy briefs, and health impact assessments

Structured analytical writing for organisations that need a piece of work done well rather than fast: literature reviews, evidence summaries, short policy briefs, health impact assessments, and submissions to consultations.

What I bring. Distinction-level academic work in each of these forms, a full Health Impact Assessment with environmental health risk assessment appendix (in support of an iwi-led fluoridation submission), a parliamentary-style submission on tobacco control, comparative health systems analysis, and policy essays on housing and Māori health. These were produced during postgraduate study, not for clients. The capability and the methodology are real; the commercial track record is what I am still building.

Best fit. Small NGOs and community groups that need analytical writing but cannot afford a large consultancy; iwi groups working on submissions; local councils on specific policy questions; small policy shops needing additional analytical capacity for a defined project.

Intro pricing for first clients. From NZD 1,500 for a short evidence review or policy brief; from NZD 2,500 for a structured HIA on a defined scope. Standard rates will be higher once I have completed paid commissions to reference.

4. Submissions and regulatory writing

Drafting and editing of submissions and regulatory documents for small health businesses that do not have an in-house regulatory function: ASA complaints and responses, Medsafe-facing communications, Health Committee submissions, Ministry of Health consultations, and internal compliance documentation.

What I bring. A Postgraduate Diploma in Public Health that included Health Protection, Public Health Policy, and Regulatory Impact Assessment training. The portfolio piece for this offer, the mock submission on smokefree legislation, was a postgraduate assignment rather than a filed submission, and I will say that clearly to any client who asks. The analytical framing, the use of evidence, and the regulatory awareness translate directly; the experience of actually filing on behalf of a client is what I am building.

Best fit. Small health and wellness businesses needing one-off submission support; small clinics responding to regulator correspondence; community groups making submissions to public consultations.

Intro pricing for first clients. From NZD 1,200 for a short submission or regulator response on a defined scope; from NZD 2,000 for a longer evidence-backed submission. Standard rates will move up as I build a track record.

How to start a conversation

If something on this page looks like it could be a fit, the most useful first step is a short email describing what you have in mind. I will reply with whether the project sounds like a fit, what I would need from you to scope it properly, and an honest read on whether I am the right person for it, including pointing you elsewhere if I am not.
