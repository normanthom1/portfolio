Blog

This is where I write about the things I find interesting on a personal level. This may be a current public health debate in Aotearoa, a reflection on Colombia, a comparison between the two countries. Some of it may be analytical, but a lot of it is less academic than the formal work page. Writing here is how I keep up with public health as it changes, how I practise my writing, and how I make sense of moving between the two countries that have shaped how I see this work. I aim to publish once a month.
